# 🛒 Global E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Behavior Analytics Dashboard

An executive business intelligence dashboard tracking multi-regional transaction volume, acquisition channels, and demographic purchasing patterns across **120,000 user sessions**, **33,600 completed orders**, and **$4.49M in gross revenue** (spanning 2020 through 2025).

## 🔍 Key Findings & Commercial Insights

### 1. Demographic Purchasing Concentration
* **55+ Age Cohort Dominance:** Customers aged **55+** represent the single largest transaction volume driver, accounting for **12,210 orders** (~36.3% of total store orders).
* **Mid-Tier Stability:** The 25–34, 45–54, and 35–44 demographics exhibit consistent purchasing momentum, generating **5,865**, **5,797**, and **5,696 orders** respectively.
* **Entry-Level Demographics:** The 18–24 cohort registered **4,012 orders**, suggesting growth potential through targeted entry-tier offerings.

### 2. Balanced Category Distribution
* Product sales across all 7 primary departments (**Beauty, Sports, Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Books, Toys, Fashion**) remain evenly distributed at **~14.3% each**, reflecting strong portfolio diversification with minimal category concentration risk.

### 3. Traffic Channel Performance & Unit Economics (US vs. GB)
* **Organic & Direct Traffic Lead:** 
  * In the **United States (US)**:
    * **Organic Search:** Generated **2,885 orders** and **$378,136.12** in spend ($9,503.56 in promotional discounts).
    * **Direct Traffic:** Generated **2,325 orders** and **$318,692.47** in spend ($8,173.16 in discounts).
  * In the **United Kingdom (GB)**:
    * **Organic Search:** Drove **1,180 orders** and **$160,372.54** in spend.
    * **Direct Traffic:** Drove **1,026 orders** and **$136,496.22** in spend.
* **Paid & Referral Channels:**
  * Paid advertising accounted for **309 orders ($41.97K spend)** in the US, indicating that organic brand equity and direct visits represent over **85%** of regional revenue volume.
  * Email marketing produced consistent conversion (**293 US orders / $42.39K spend**; **149 GB orders / $18.63K spend**).

## 🛠️ Analytics Architecture & Dimensional Controls

* **Interactive Dimension Slicers:** Dynamic filtering across **Country**, **Top Category**, **Preferred Source**, and multi-year **Date Range**.
* **Cohort & Funnel Modeling:** Tracking session-to-order progression and cart abandonment volume (**3.7K abandoned units**).
* **Multi-Currency & Regional Segmentation:** Country-level variance modeling comparing conversion channels, total spend, discount utilization, and customer ratings.
